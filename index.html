THE CHALLENGE

Medical Waste Needs Better Visibility

Hospitals generate different categories of biomedical waste every day. MediSafe focuses on making the workflow clear and trackable.

What happens without a connected system?

Manual records, incorrect segregation, delayed pickup information and limited staff feedback can make waste management harder to monitor.

♻️ Segregation Different waste categories can be mixed if guidance is unclear.
📋 Records Paper-based records provide limited real-time visibility.
🚛 Collection Pickup status can be difficult to follow across departments.
👥 Staff Engagement Good segregation practices need continuous training and recognition.

MediSafe is a smart medical-waste management platform designed to make segregation, tracking, collection and staff engagement easier.


Our Principle

🟡 Yellow

Mainly applicable anatomical, soiled, pharmaceutical and specified laboratory waste.

  • Anatomical waste
  • Blood-stained cotton
  • Dressings and bandages
  • Applicable expired medicines
  • Specified laboratory waste

🔴 Red

Contaminated recyclable plastic waste.

  • IV sets
  • Tubing
  • Catheters
  • Urine bags
  • Plastic gloves

⚪ White

Sharps requiring puncture-resistant containers.

  • Needles
  • Fixed-needle syringes
  • Scalpels
  • Blades
  • Metal sharps

🔵 Blue

Glassware and specified metallic implants.

  • Glass vials
  • Ampoules
  • Broken glass
  • Unbroken glassware
  • Metallic implants

Important Segregation Rules


Never mix: Keep waste categories separated at source.


Track: Use appropriate barcode/QR identification according to facility procedures.


Protect staff: Follow approved PPE and handling procedures.
